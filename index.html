Hi, I'm Nathan. I'm in the final stretch of my MS in Statistics at UC Irvine. This summer I was a Fraud and Risk Analyst Intern at Intuit, where I built internal Claude Skills to automate fraud and risk policy workflows and improved fraud detection models for Credit Karma Money. Before that I was at Tarsus Pharmaceuticals, building Databricks pipelines, wiring up MCP servers to Microsoft Copilot Studio, and helping the commercial team make sense of their data.
