About me

Based in San Mateo, studying and working in Irvine.

Hi, I'm Nathan. I'm in the final stretch of my MS in Statistics at UC Irvine while working full-time at Tarsus Pharmaceuticals. At work I build Databricks pipelines, wire up MCP servers to Microsoft Copilot Studio, and help the commercial team make sense of their data.

On the academic side I've done a lot of Bayesian modeling, causal inference, and clinical stats. My qualifying exam was a full RCT analysis — 298 people, mixed-effects models, multiple imputation, ended up with R²=0.87 predicting week 2 activity. That kind of work is what I find genuinely interesting.

Looking for roles in data science or ML engineering where the work is technically serious and ships to production.
