A bit about me

Hi, I'm Nathan. I'm finishing a Master's in Statistics at UC Irvine, where my work has ranged from Bayesian spatial models to building production RAG chatbots. I like problems where getting the statistics wrong actually matters.

On the applied side, I interned at Tarsus Pharmaceuticals building an enterprise RAG system on Databricks, and I've spent time doing AI evaluation and data annotation work. On the research side I've worked on clinical biostatistics, causal inference, and longitudinal modeling.

I'm looking for data science and ML roles where I can do work that's technically rigorous and actually ships.
